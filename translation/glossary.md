# Deltora Quest DS glossary

**Source key**
- **W**: spelling verified on Wikipedia.
- **G**: confirmed by the game itself (internal class names, in-game text).
- **M**: book spelling from memory, not yet verified against a source.
- **T**: translated term, or a game-only name, chosen for this project.
- **?**: unresolved, waiting on a decision (see the end).

Style: English book spellings beat literal Japanese. British/Australian spelling where it's the books' own ("Grey"). The tone suits younger readers, as the original does.
